What is Idul Fitri?

It is the day of celebration right after Ramadan ends: 1 Syawal. Tap each word to find out what it means.

So Eid al-Fitr is a day to thank Allah for helping us finish Ramadan.

The First Eid

How do we know it’s Eid?

Looking for the Moon

A new Islamic month starts when we see the hilal, the thin new crescent moon, after sunset. It’s the 29th night of Ramadan. Tap the clouds to find it!

Where is the hilal?

Then we fast one more day, so Ramadan has 30 days. The Prophet SAW said: “Fast when you see it, and stop fasting when you see it. If it is cloudy, complete thirty days.” Sahih al-Bukhari 1909

Before we celebrate

Zakat al-Fitr

Food we give to people in need, so that everyone can be happy on Eid. It is paid for every person in the family. Tap each bag to put it in the basket.

    The Takbir

    “Allah is the Greatest. There is no god but Allah. Allah is the Greatest, and all praise belongs to Allah.” We say it because Allah told us to “glorify Allah for guiding you, and perhaps you will be grateful.” QS. Al-Baqarah 2:185

    Back to our fitrah

    Saying Sorry & Starting Fresh

    Fitrah means our pure, clean nature. Whoever fasts Ramadan with faith gets their past sins forgiven (Bukhari 38). But mistakes with people, we fix ourselves. Tap each smudge to say sorry and clean the page!

    Eid greeting

    تَقَبَّلَ اللهُ مِنَّا وَمِنْكُمْ

    Taqabbalallahu minna wa minkum

    “May Allah accept our good deeds and yours.” This is what the Sahabah said to each other on Eid. In Indonesia we also do maaf-maafan.
