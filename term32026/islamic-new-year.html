The story of the Hijrah

In Makkah, many people worshipped idols, and they hurt the Muslims for many years.

The Quraysh made a plan to harm the Prophet ﷺ. At night, they waited outside his house.

Allah told him to go. 'Ali slept in his bed, and the Prophet ﷺ left safely with Abu Bakr.

They hid in the Cave of Thawr for three nights.

Then they travelled to Madinah. The Muslims there waited every morning, and welcomed him with joy!

What can we learn from Hijrah?

Trusting Allah in times of difficulty brings ease

In the cave, the Prophet ﷺ said: "Do not be sad. Allah is with us."

With difficulty comes ease

After the hard years in Makkah, Allah gave them a safe home in Madinah.

فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا

Brotherhood / Sisterhood

The people of Madinah (the Ansar) shared their homes with the people from Makkah (the Muhajirun), like brothers and sisters.
