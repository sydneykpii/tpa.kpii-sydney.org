What is Nuzulul Qur'an?

What? The first revelation

The first words of the Qur'an came down from Allah to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. Nuzul means "coming down".

When & where? Ramadan · Cave of Hira

On a night in Ramadan, remembered on 17 Ramadan, inside the Cave of Hira near Makkah.

The story

Long, long ago, in Makkah...

There lived Muhammad ﷺ

A kind and honest man. People called him Al-Amin, the trustworthy.

The time of Jahiliyah...

Many people in Makkah worshipped idols and forgot Allah. The Prophet ﷺ was sad to see this.

Jabal an-Nur, the Mountain of Light

So he climbed a mountain near Makkah and sat alone in a small cave, Hira, to think and worship Allah.

Inside Cave Hira

A special night

One night in RamadanThe Prophet ﷺ was alone in the cave. Suddenly, Angel Jibril عليه السلام came to him.

Jibril saidاِقْرَأْ "Iqra'!" Read!

The Prophet ﷺ answered"I do not know how to read." Jibril held him tightly, then said again: "Read!" This happened three times.

Then Jibril recitedاقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ "Read, in the name of your Lord who created." These were the first words of the Qur'an!

Words essence

Islam puts learning and reading above everything else. The very first command from Allah was to read!

Historical context

The first revelation did not start with a command to pray or give zakat, but with the command to read and learn.

Surah Al-'Alaq 96:1-5

Two kinds of reading

What does reading actually mean?

Reading Qauliyah Ayah

Reading and understanding the words of the Qur'an.

Reading Kauniyah Ayah

Looking at the world around us: the sky, the sea, plants and animals, as signs of Allah's power (tadabbur alam).

Scientific method

"Iqra" invites us to observe, ask questions and investigate. That is how scientists learn too!
