The tenth day

What is the day of 'Asyura?

'Asyura (عاشوراء) literally means "the tenth" and refers to the 10th day of Muharram, the first month in the Islamic Hijri calendar.

It comes from the Arabic word عَشَرَة ('asyarah), which means the number 10.

So what makes it special?

The Virtues of 'Asyura

Allah split the Red Sea for Prophet Musa and his followers when they were chased by Fir'aun (the Pharaoh).

'Asyura is in Muharram, which is one of the four sacred months in Islam.

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ encourages Muslims to fast during 'Asyura.

Fasting on 'Asyura

The Benefits of Fasting on the day of 'Asyura

Fasting in Muharram ('Asyura is the 10th of Muharram) is the best fasting after Ramadhan.

Fasting 'Asyura forgives our minor sins for the past one year.

Fasting 'Asyura was made obligatory before fasting Ramadhan was prescribed.
