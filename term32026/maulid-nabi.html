Mount Safa Call

One day, the Prophet gathered the people of Quraysh at Mount Safa and asked:

"O people of Quraysh, if I told you that an army is coming at you from behind this mountain, would you believe me?"
Prophet Muhammad SAW

What do you think they answered?

They all said: "Yes! We have never heard you tell a lie."

Everyone in Makkah knew he was honest and truthful, with no exception. He lived a pure and simple life, and even his strongest enemies agreed he was the most truthful and honest person.
